Thakar Group / About

A family house of institutions

The Thakar Group is the family institutional group of the Thakar family. It holds and stewards companies across healthcare, technology, insurance, sport, shipping and real estate.

Our approach

One house, many companies

Each company in the group runs independently, with its own management and its own customers. What they share is an owner that thinks in decades.

The family sits above the operating companies as a common holding. It sets direction, allocates capital and appoints the people who lead each business. Day-to-day decisions stay with the teams who know their markets.

Where it helps, the companies share what they build: technology, know-how and relationships. Our shared AI layer is one example of that.
